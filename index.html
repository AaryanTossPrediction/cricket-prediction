📘 LBW Rule Explained (Leg Before Wicket)

The LBW rule exists to prevent batters from using their body instead of the bat to stop the ball from hitting the stumps.


Key Factors for Out:

  • Pitching: Ball must pitch in line or outside off stump.
  • Impact: Ball must hit pad in line with stumps.
  • Wickets: Ball trajectory must show it was going on to hit the stumps.

📘 Retired Hurt vs Retired Out

Retired Hurt: Occurs when a batter leaves the field due to illness or injury. They can return to bat later in the innings.


Retired Out: Occurs when a batter leaves without injury or permission. They cannot return to bat unless the opposing captain agrees.
